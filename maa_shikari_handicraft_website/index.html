01 — OUR HERITAGE

FROM THE HILLS OF SERAAJ

More than a craft.
A living heritage.

At Maa Shikari Handicraft Handloom, we celebrate the colours, textures and traditions that make Himachali craft distinctive. From woven shawls to traditional caps and everyday handloom pieces, our collection reflects the spirit of the mountains.

Located in Janjehli, in the beautiful Mandi district of Himachal Pradesh, we welcome you to explore pieces inspired by regional culture and timeless craftsmanship.
